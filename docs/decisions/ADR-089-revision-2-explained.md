## Your clean-pocket checklist

While the pocket is clean and his count or reads run out:

1. **Checkdown,** today's rule for who, competing with his other men. A ball goes only to a man at 30 or more on the game's 0 to 100 openness scale, no desperation ball at 25 (D23).
2. **One pick: move or break out** (D27); no standing still, no throwaway in the pocket. Poised, he moves first; rattled, he may break out first.
3. **Pocket movement:** a climb or slide; his reads restart with about a second more, and a receiver past his break with a trailing or level defender gains a step: your "window".
4. **Scramble drill:** each receiver gets a fresh chance against his man, more on the side he runs to (each route's lane on the play card gives its side).
5. **Run or throw it away** when the chase arrives: mobile runs, pocket passer throws it away.

Once the pocket is pressured it does not start; a seen rusher within half a second ends it (must act). Gone wrong, it is your coverage sack.

## The alarm and contact

Each rusher carries how he comes, his side, his speed and whether he was seen early, late or never (rolled on awareness; a bull rush always is). **At the alarm** he picks from that rusher's list: bull rush, keep reading or slide; corner, step up; straight through, slide, run or make him miss; free runner, make him miss, run or throw. Rattled, he may choose worse. **Making him miss:** once a play; won, the rusher flies past; lost, he is hit at once. **Contact:** his strength and agility against tackling and speed; a man still blocked favours him; one nobody blocked, from behind or never seen, the rusher.
