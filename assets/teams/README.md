# League team marks

The 32 team marks for the league, one per team, with each team's two approved colors in `manifest.json`.

- Real NFL cities, our own names, colors and marks. No real team names, logos or likenesses.
- Files are 512 x 512 PNGs with see-through backgrounds, cut out from the owner's image-tool originals (1024 or 2048 px, white background, kept on the owner's computer).
- Ids (T01 to T32) match `packages/attributes/leagues/standin-2026-10-23.json`. That file still carries the older team names; renaming them there is a separate step.
- Putting colors and marks into the shared team record is a contracts change and needs its own petition.

Order: T01-T04 AFC North, T05-T08 AFC South, T09-T12 AFC East, T13-T16 AFC West, T17-T20 NFC North, T21-T24 NFC South, T25-T28 NFC East, T29-T32 NFC West.
