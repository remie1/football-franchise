The office and the broadcast

Our art plan says the game has two looks. The office is your own building: quiet, matte, paper and painted block. The broadcast is how the public sees football: dark, loud, high contrast.

The written rules live in assets/style/STYLE_GUIDE.md. The teams, names and numbers here are made-up samples.

    3. How a team's colors fit in

    Real NFL cities, our own names, colors and marks. Each team gets two colors and a simple mark; the brighter one draws its bars on the broadcast. A score or a turnover always looks the same, whoever is playing.

    The owner's picks

    1. Two looks: approved.
    2. Fonts: typewriter in the office, Barlow Condensed on the broadcast.
    3. Green for a score or a win, red for a turnover: keep.
    4. Box score: quarters under FINAL with no total column; team totals with logos and bars split by the raw numbers; leaders side by side with big numbers; a scoring summary for every quarter with drive lines and the running score.
    5. Teams: real NFL cities, our own names, colors and marks.
